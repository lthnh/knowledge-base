First steps in research process:
- Define the problem
- Develop research objectives
- Develop hypotheses

The hardest part in research is often the problem statement. The problem is not clearly defined, can have different interpretation which leads to different approach to resolve the problem.

Once finish defining problem, next we need to care about research methods. Be careful at this step as unsuitable research method can lead to your paper being rejected.

Finally, you need to define what results do you expect.
