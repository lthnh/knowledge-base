## Analog signal
Analog signal can be considered a continuous function of time. The signal itself corresponds directly with the information it represents.
## Digital signal
Digital signal is discrete with respect to time. In digital circuit it represents by 0 and 1. The transition between the two states is considered instant thus the digital signal is discrete (this is not true in practice).
The information is encoded (i.e. in digital circuit it's binary encoding).
## Why Digital signal is preferred over Analog signal
Digital signal can operate in the presence of noise. The reason for this is analog signal is the direct representation of the information it carries, any noise coupled into signal is interpreted as noise on the original physical quantity. While digital signal can be recovered as long as the noise don't exceed its threshold since digital signal is discrete.
Digital circuit is simpler since you only need a switch to change from 0 to 1 and reverse. Analog circuit is much more complex.
Digital circuit has lower energy consumption than analog circuit.
