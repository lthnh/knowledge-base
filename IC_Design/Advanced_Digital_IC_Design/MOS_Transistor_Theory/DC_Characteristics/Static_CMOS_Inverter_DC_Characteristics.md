Ideal transistors are only in region C for $V_{in}$ = $V_{DD}$/2 and the slope of this transfer curve corresponding to -$\infty$ gain due to infinite output resistance.

>[!note]
>Real transistors have finite output resistance due to channel length modulation, thus have finite slopes over a broader region C.

![[stimulated-cmos-inverter-65nm.png|200]]

This is stimulation result of 65 nm process CMOS inverter. The crossover point where $V_{inv}$ = $V_{in}$ = $V_{out}$ is called the *input threshold*.
Because both mobility and threshold voltage decrease with temperature, input threshold is weakly dependent on temperature.
