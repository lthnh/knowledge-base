I will try installing Xyce as complement to NGspice. Installing Xyce via Spack has resulted in an compilation error of Trilinos dependency. This happens to AUR too. The most efficient approach to installing Xyce would be compiling it from source on Debian.

When building Xyce from source, Xyce can't find AMD include/library directories and must be explicitly included in the configure command:

```cmake
-D TPL_AMD_INCLUDE_DIRS=/usr/include/suitesparse \
-D TPL_AMD_LIBRARIES=/usr/lib/x86_64-linux-gnu/libamd.so
```
