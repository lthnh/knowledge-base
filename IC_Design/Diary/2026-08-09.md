Today, while implementing testbenches for various submodules I meet a few problems. Firstly, Verilog 2005 doesn't support increment/decrement operator. Every increment/decrement statments must be written out explicitly (e.g. i = i+1). For the next project, I will definitely move to SystemVerilog for better ergonomics. Secondly, reg can't be used to drive continuous assignments in Verilog 2005. I am surprised to know that this is allowed in SystemVerilog. But since I have committed to Verilog 2005 (for now). I will keep it Verilog 2005 compatible. Thirdly, Makefile vpath command doesn't support directories separated by comma. You must separate paths with space, otherwise it will not find prerequisites.
