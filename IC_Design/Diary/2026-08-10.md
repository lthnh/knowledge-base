Also, when implementing subtraction with adder circuit, I notice that the borrow bit is 1 when a > b. This is CORRECT behaviour since $(~b+1)$ is equivalent to $2^n-b$.And $a-b = a-(~b+1) = a-b+2^n$. For $a-b>0$, overflow definitely occurs.
