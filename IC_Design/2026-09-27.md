What is WBSS? WBSS stands for Worst Bus Skew Slack. Violation means all bits on the same bus don't arrive at the same time.
What is WPWS? WPWS stands for Worst Pulse Width Slack. It's the minimum pulse-width in pulse-width checks. $\text{Pulse-width slack} = T_{\text{actual pulse}} - T_{\text{required pulse}}$.
What is TPWS? TPWS stands for Total Pulse Width Slack. It's the total sum of all pulse-width slacks.
When met with failed timing due to unsatisfied TPWS. You can increase the $T_{\text{constraint}}$ to satisfy it.
Duty cycle is calculated as the percentage of HIGH signal duration to the total period.

How can people measure resource usage on FPGA? They will measure on how many LUTs, FFs, DSP, BRAM, etc. that you use. To compare between different FPGA types, they use hardware utilization as a measure (?).

Criterias for satisfying time closure:
- WNS >= 0
- WHS >= 0
- TPWS = 0
