## I-V Characteristic

![[ideal-diode-i-v-characteristic.png|500]]

Diode has two terminals: Anode for positive terminal and Cathode for negative terminal.

If a positive voltage is applied between the diode, or the diode is forward-biased, the diode is short and any mount of current can pass through it.
If a negative voltage is applied between the diode, or the diode is reverse-biased, the diode is open and no current flow through it.

The ideal I-V characteristic curve is *piece-wise linear*.

>[!important]
This piece-wise linear assumption is only valid when the signal across its terminals swings along only one of the linear segments.
$\Rightarrow$ If is signal passes through one or more break points in the characteristic, the assumption no longer hold.

The current must be limited by an external circuit.
