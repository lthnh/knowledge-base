The very steep I-V curve in the breakdown region of diode can be exploited in voltage regulator designs.
The diodes that are made specifically for exploiting this region are *breakdown diodes*, or *zener diodes*.
This is its symbol:

![[zener-diodes.png|100]]

In normal applications, the current flows from cathode to anode. Cathode is positive with respect to anode. The current and voltage are both positive.
