$V_t$ typically increases with channel length.
Therefore in small L transistors, where the source and drain regions extend into a significant portion of the channel, $V_t$ decreases.
This is called *short channel effect* or $V_t$ *roll-off*.
In some processes, a *reverse short channel effect* causes $V_t$ to decrease with length.

There is also *narrow channel effect* in which $V_t$ varies with channel width. But this effect is less significant as the minimum channel width is greater than the minimum channel length.
