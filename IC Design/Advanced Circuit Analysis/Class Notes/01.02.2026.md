# 1A. Diode Model

## General
Diodes are p-n junction. Created by putting p and n regions next to each other.
Two main types: typical (chỉnh lưu), zener (voltage regulator)
Annotations: Anode (A), Cathode (K).
## I-V Characteristics
Three regions of operation: forward, reverse and breakdown.
Breakdown region is utilized for voltage regulation applications.
Put a voltage between the two terminal of a diode means biasing it.
With positive voltage connect to the p region and ground to the n region, we forward bias the diode. Putting voltage in the opposite fashion results in reverse biasing the diode.
## Diode models
Three models:
- Exponential model
- Small signal model
- Ideal (voltage source)
