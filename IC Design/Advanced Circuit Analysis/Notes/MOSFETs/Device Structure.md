The n-type MOSFET is fabricated on a p-type substrate. There are two heavily doped n-type regions: *source* and *drain*. A thin layer of silicon dioxide is grown between the n-type regions. A metal gate is deposited on the silicon dioxide layer to form *gate electrode*. Metal contacts are made to the source, drain, the substrate (or the *body*).

>[!note]
>Most modern MOSFETs are fabricated using a process known as silicon-gate technology, which uses polysilicon to form the gate electrode.

Another name for MOSFET is the *insulated-gate FET* or *IGFET*.

The substrate forms the pn junctions with the source and drain. In the case of n-type MOSFET, these junctions are two back-to-back diodes.

There is also p-type MOSFET. We inverse the type of source, drain and substrate of n-type to get p-type.
