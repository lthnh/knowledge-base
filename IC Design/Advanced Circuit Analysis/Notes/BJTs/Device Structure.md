This is simplified structure of pnp transistor.

The n type BJT consists of three regions: the emitter region (n type), the base region (p type), and the collector region (n type). The opposite is for p type BJT.

Respectively, terminals are labeled *emitter* (E), *base* (B), and *collector* (C).

As we can see from the simplified structure of BJT, the transistor consists of two pn junctions: the *emitter-base junction* (EBJ) and the *collector-base junction* (CBJ). This will determine the modes of operation of BJT.
