| Active     | Forward                     | Reverse                       |
| Saturation | Forward                     | Forward                       |
**Modes of operation of BJT table.**

The *active mode* is used when transistor is operate as an amplifier.
Switching applications use both *cutoff mode* and *saturation mode*.
In cutoff mode there is no current flows since both junctions are revers-biased.

Charge carriers from both polarities participate in the current-conducting process, hence the name *bipolar*.
