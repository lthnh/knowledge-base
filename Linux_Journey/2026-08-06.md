There are 2 problems that I met during the time using immutable distro from Fedora:
1. Incorrect .desktop export by Distrobox causes UniFlash unable to startup.
2. Install cloudflare-warp but its service has not been enabled during setup. Must be enable explicitly with `sudo systemctl enable --now warp-svc.sevice`.
