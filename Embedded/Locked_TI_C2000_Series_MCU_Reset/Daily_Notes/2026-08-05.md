Today, I try to connect with JTAG interface of the ECU. It fails at first with *wrong configuration* error. The error code is 1135. After testing with jtagdebug cmd, I found out that it is the isolation IC that causes this problem. Short through them will solve this error and we successfully connect to the chip through JTAG.
